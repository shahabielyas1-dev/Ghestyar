Ghestyar 4.7.0 — Step 4.1: multi-account cashflow foundation

Implemented:
- Added up to 50 accounts with types bank, cash, wallet and other.
- Legacy cashSettings migrate to permanent cash-main account without changing its amount or balance date.
- Legacy income/expense entries and installment payments without accountId are attributed to cash-main.
- Added account create/edit/archive controls in cashflow page.
- Main account cannot be archived; at least one active account is required.
- Accounts with linked ledger entries, active installment payments or non-zero current balance cannot be archived.
- Income/expense form selects the receiving/payment account.
- Installment payment form selects the paying account.
- Account current balances are calculated independently from the snapshot and later linked transactions/payments.
- Six-month forecast aggregates active accounts and account-linked future income/expenses.
- Forecast does not claim a reliable total projected balance if any active account has no configured balance snapshot.
- Account registry is persisted in local storage, cloud snapshots/cache, emergency checkpoints, backup export and restore preview.
- cashSettings remains synchronized with cash-main for compatibility.

Tests:
- All four inline JavaScript blocks pass Node.js syntax validation.
- Legacy payment migration test passed.
- Legacy cash balance and transaction migration test passed.
- Two-account balance test passed: main 7,500,000 + wallet 3,000,000 = 10,500,000.
- Forecast aggregation test passed: opening 12,000,000; forecast income 2,000,000; expense 1,000,000; installments 1,000,000; ending 12,000,000.
- Invalid account data rejection passed.
- Static checks confirmed account selectors, modal rendering, local/cloud/backup persistence and restore integration.
- ZIP integrity check passed.
