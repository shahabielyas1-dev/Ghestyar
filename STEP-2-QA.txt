Ghestyar 4.6.0 — Step 2 / Issue 1
Fix: date-anchored cash balance

What changed
- Added cashSettings.balanceDate.
- The saved balance is defined as the end-of-day balance for the selected Persian-calendar date.
- Legacy backups without balanceDate remain importable; their balanceDate defaults to today to avoid retroactively applying unknown transactions.
- Cash ledger entries and non-reversed installment payments after the anchor date through today are rolled into the estimated current balance.
- With a configured balance, future cash-ledger forecast entries begin tomorrow, preventing same-day double counting.
- The cash-flow page displays the estimated current balance and allows selecting the anchor date with the existing Persian date picker.
- Future anchor dates are disabled and rejected.
- The anchor date is included in local storage, backup snapshots, and the existing Supabase wallet payload through the existing cashSettings object.

Automated checks
- All inline JavaScript blocks passed `node --check`.
- Headless Chromium finance scenario passed:
  - historical one-time income added to the anchored balance
  - historical one-time expense subtracted from the anchored balance
  - non-reversed installment payment after the anchor subtracted from the balance
  - reversed payment ignored
  - future recurring income included in the forecast
  - future one-time expense included in the forecast
  - unpaid arrears remain in the current-month installment requirement
  - partially paid installment uses its remaining amount
  - six-month projection accumulates balances correctly
  - legacy cashSettings without balanceDate migrates to today's date
- Headless Chromium UI scenario passed:
  - Persian date picker opens from the balance-date field
  - future date is disabled
  - a past date can be selected
  - balance and selected date save together to local storage
  - no browser runtime exceptions in the tested flow

Important semantics
- The entered balance must be the balance at the END of the selected day.
- Existing records with no date are assigned today's date on first load. If the stored balance was actually captured earlier, select that historical date and save the balance again.
- These tests do not verify live Supabase credentials, remote writes, push notifications, or installation behavior.
